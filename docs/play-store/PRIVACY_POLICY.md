Last updated: November 2026

LocalMiles ("the app") helps delivery teams dispatch jobs, drivers complete deliveries and clients track shipments.
This policy explains what information the app uses.

## Information we collect

| Information | Why |
|---|---|
| Name, email address and profile picture from your Google account | To sign you in and show who you are |
| User ID and role (dispatcher, driver or client) | To show you the right screens and jobs |
| Precise location (drivers only) | To share your position with the dispatcher while a job is on the way, and to confirm you are at the stop when you submit proof of delivery |
| Photos and signature images (drivers) | To record proof of delivery |
| Delivery information you enter (addresses, recipient names, notes) | To run delivery jobs |
| Device push notification token | To send you notifications about your jobs |

We do not use advertising or analytics, and we do not sell your information.

## Location

Location is only read when a driver shares their location for a job or submits a proof of delivery. The app asks
for permission first. You can turn location off in your phone's settings, but drivers cannot complete proof of
delivery without it.

## Biometrics

Fingerprint and face unlock are handled by Android. The app never receives or stores your fingerprint or face data;
it only learns whether the check succeeded.

## Where information is stored

- Account sign-in and push notifications: Google Firebase
- Delivery data, photos and signatures: Supabase (database and file storage)
- API hosting: Render

Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Some delivery changes are stored on your phone until you are
online again, then sent to the server.

## Sharing

Information is shared only inside the delivery team that uses the app (for example a client sees their own shipment
and a dispatcher sees their drivers) and with the service providers listed above, who process it for us.
