## Full description (max 4000)

LocalMiles keeps small courier teams, their drivers and their customers on the same page.

One app, three roles:

DISPATCHERS
• Create delivery jobs with several stops and assign a driver and vehicle
• Manage your fleet and drivers, and promote users to drivers in one step
• See your drivers on a live map
• Review delivery history

DRIVERS
• See your assigned jobs and work through each stop in order
• Capture proof of delivery: recipient name, photo, signature and your location
• Keep working with no signal. Your updates are saved on the phone and sent automatically when you are back online
• Share your live location while a job is on the way
• Open the next stop in your favourite navigation app

CLIENTS
• Follow your own shipments stop by stop
• Get notified when something changes

SECURE AND SIMPLE
• Sign in with Google
• Unlock the app with your fingerprint or face
• Light, dark or system theme
• Available in English, Afrikaans and isiZulu

LocalMiles was built as a student project for South African delivery operators who need real-time visibility without enterprise pricing.
